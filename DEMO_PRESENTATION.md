# Aravat Landscaping — Independent AI Receptionist Concept Demo

This is an **independent, unsolicited sales demonstration**, not an official Aravat Landscaping website or a deployed customer service system. Aravat Landscaping has not commissioned this work.

## The 3-minute owner walkthrough

1. Open the homepage. Explain: "Your existing website can keep doing what it does. This demonstrates an AI assistant that could help visitors turn questions into organized project inquiries."
2. Select **Plan Your Project With AI**. Use a fictional scenario: "I'm considering a paver patio in Palo Alto, about 500 square feet, within the next three months. What should I consider?"
3. Let the assistant ask about the project. Do **not** enter real contact details.
4. Open **View Demo Inquiry Inbox**. Review the fictional sample inquiries or a session-only inquiry generated during the walkthrough.
5. Explain the possible paid implementation: connect a production assistant to the client's approved lead inbox and consultation process, with consent, security, monitoring, and real testing.

## What is real versus simulated?

- The frontend and AI chat endpoint are implemented in code; their availability must be tested in the deployed preview.
- Demo inquiry records live only in the current browser session. They disappear on refresh.
- The inbox starts with clearly labeled fictional examples.
- The demo does not accept real lead submissions through the backend.
- The demo does not confirm appointments, send real consultation requests, or connect to the client's private systems.
- Portfolio stock photos are inspiration, **not** verified work by Aravat Landscaping.
- The independent-concept notice must remain visible.
